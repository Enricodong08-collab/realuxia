创始人故事The Founder Narrative

橙色印记理性力量与心灵觉知The Heritage of OrangeRational Mastery, and Spiritual Awakening

在 REALUXIA 的黑白世界里，那一抹橙色是唯一的呼吸与温度。

Within the quiet monochrome world of REALUXIA, orange is the singular breath of light and vitality.

橙色代表着源源不竭的生命力。它提醒每个人勇敢活出本真，坦然拥抱未知。

这份色彩更深植于最纯粹的爱。创始人的女儿出生于加州橙县，让爱与橙色成为她一生的精神底色，驱使她十二年来始终笃信真理与真爱。

这正是 REALUXIA 的缘起。

支撑这份热忱的，是理性与感性的深层融合。

中欧国际工商学院 Global EMBA 与麻省理工学院供应链管理的学术底盘，赋予了她构建全球贸易网络时近乎苛刻的商业严谨与风控智慧。

而心理学博士领域的探索，则让她洞悉了奢品背后的精神内核。奢品从来不止于物质，更关乎审美自省、心灵愉悦与自我实现。

走过十二载风雨，她深知在这个行业前行的壁垒。

她选择将自身沉淀的成熟供应链、合规通路与专业经验全面开放，赋能更多像自己与女儿一样热爱生活、心怀梦想的人。

让每一位同行者都能依托这一高起点，以合法合规、从容优雅且卓越高效的方式，成就属于自己的奢品事业，共同拥抱世界之美。

It embodies boundless life energy, inspiring every individual to live authentically and embrace the unknown.

This color also carries the deepest personal devotion. Her daughter was born in Orange County, California, forever tying the warmth of orange and unconditional love into the fabric of her life, anchoring her twelve year journey in the relentless pursuit of truth and love.

This conviction defines the creation of REALUXIA.

Sustaining this devotion is a rare synthesis of intellectual rigor and emotional depth.

Executive training from the CEIBS Global EMBA and advanced Supply Chain Management at MIT provide the structural precision, agility, and risk discipline required for cross border commerce.

Simultaneously, her doctoral inquiries in psychology uncover the spiritual essence beneath luxury fashion. It is never merely about commodities, but an elevation of aesthetics, inner joy, and self realization.

Having navigated the industry for twelve years, she understands both its barriers and its transformative power.

Her mission is to channel this foundation of supply chain strength, absolute compliance, and professional insight into an open platform that empowers others.

She seeks to uplift dreamers who, like herself and her daughter, cherish beauty and life, enabling them to build their own luxury ventures with legal integrity, effortless elegance, and commercial distinction, coming together to embrace the beauty of the world.
