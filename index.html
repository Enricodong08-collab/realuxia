The Founder Narrative創始人故事

The Heritage of Orange, Rational Mastery, and Spiritual Awakening橙色印記
理性力量與心靈覺知

Within the quiet monochrome world of REALUXIA, orange is the singular breath of light and vitality. It embodies boundless life energy, inspiring every individual to live authentically and embrace the unknown. This color also carries the deepest personal devotion. Her daughter was born in Orange County, California, forever tying the warmth of orange and unconditional love into the fabric of her life, anchoring her lifelong journey in the relentless pursuit of truth and love. This conviction defines the creation of REALUXIA.

Sustaining this devotion is a rare synthesis of intellectual rigor and emotional depth. Executive training from the CEIBS Global EMBA and advanced Supply Chain Management at MIT provide the structural precision, agility, and risk discipline required for cross border commerce.

Simultaneously, her doctoral inquiries in psychology uncover the spiritual essence beneath luxury fashion. It is never merely about commodities, but an elevation of aesthetics, inner joy, and self realization.

Having navigated the industry for over a decade, she understands both its barriers and its transformative power. Her mission is to channel this foundation of supply chain strength, absolute compliance, and professional insight into an open platform that empowers others. She seeks to uplift dreamers who, like herself and her daughter, cherish beauty and life, enabling them to build their own luxury ventures with legal integrity, effortless elegance, and commercial distinction, coming together to embrace the beauty of the world.

在 REALUXIA 的黑白世界裡，那一抹橙色是唯一的呼吸與溫度。橙色代表著源源不竭的生命力。它提醒每個人勇敢活出本真，坦然擁抱未知。這份色彩更深植於最純粹的愛。創始人的女兒出生於加州橙縣，讓愛與橙色成為她一生的精神底色，驅使她始終篤信真理與真愛。這正是 REALUXIA 的緣起。

支撐這份熱忱的，是理性與感性的深層融合。中歐國際工商學院 Global EMBA 與麻省理工學院供應鏈管理的學術底盤，賦予了她構建全球貿易網路時近乎苛刻的商業嚴謹與風控智慧。

而心理學博士領域的探索，則讓她洞悉了奢品背後的精神內核。奢品從來不止於物質，更關乎審美自省、心靈愉悅與自我實現。

走過十餘載風雨，她深知在這個行業前行的壁壘。她選擇將自身沉澱的成熟供應鏈、合規通路與專業經驗全面開放，賦能更多像自己與女兒一樣熱愛生活、心懷夢想的人。讓每一位同行者都能依託這一高起點，以合法合規、從容優雅且卓越高效的方式，成就屬於自己的奢品事業，共同擁抱世界之美。
